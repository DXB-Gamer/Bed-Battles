Bed Battles Arcade 2D · v4.0.0

A Bedwars-style brawl. Protect your bed, collect resources at generators, bridge across the void and destroy enemy beds.

Move: WASD · Aim: mouse · Left click: attack / break · Right click (or Shift+click): place block · T: TNT · F: Fireball · R: Ender Pearl · E or wheel: change block type
Stand on generators to collect. Near your shop/upgrade NPC use the hotkeys shown (letters / 3–7 shown on buttons) or click the buttons.
